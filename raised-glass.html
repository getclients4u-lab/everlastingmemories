Do I have to read it word for word?

No — but you shouldn't memorize it either. Read it once into the teleprompter and let it scroll at your pace, or print the pocket card and keep it in your palm. The best speakers look up between beats: greeting, story, heart, raise — four glances, four breaths, done.

How long should a toast actually be?

45 to 90 seconds — about 100 to 200 words at a natural 130 wpm. Isabella's notebook rule: three toasts max, and the fourth toast is where timelines go to die. If your speech needs more than 150 seconds, it's not a toast anymore; it's a keynote, and the cake is getting cold.

What if I'm not funny?

Then pick Heartfelt and stop worrying. The room never remembers whether you made them laugh; they remember whether you made them feel something. Warmth lands every time — humor is just the garnish.

Can it write a roast?

The Playful tone gives you roast-with-a-hug — a wink, never a wound. Real roasts need a decade of trust behind them. If you have to ask whether the honoree can take it, they can't. Keep it kind; the group chat can be ruthless later.

Why does a balloon company have a toast writer?

Because Isabella stands through the toast block at every wedding she decorates — 60+ weddings, 400+ toasts — and she's seen the room go quiet for the good ones and die for the long ones. She decorates the room and she knows what happens in it. The Party Day Timeline schedules the toast block before the dance floor opens for the same reason.
