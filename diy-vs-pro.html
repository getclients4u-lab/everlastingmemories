💡 The honest truth about DIY balloons

We're a small studio — we'd rather win your trust than your ignorance.

💰 Retail markup is real — Party City charges $6–8 per helium balloon. Our bouquets use premium latex at a fraction of that, because we buy in pro bulk.
🕑 Time is the hidden cost — a 10 ft organic garland is ~80 balloons. At 2 minutes each, that's nearly 3 hours of inflating before you even hang it.
💥 The oops factor — first-timers pop or mis-size 1 in 4 balloons. That's 20 extra balloons, an extra store trip, and a little piece of your soul.
💧 Helium math — a tank rental runs ~$55 and 80% of it floats away unused if you over-order. Pros bring exactly what's needed.
🙌 Sunday is sacred — DIY setups steal the day BEFORE your event, and the morning OF. You're the host — you should be getting ready, not getting dizzy.
🎉 When DIY wins — small, casual, under ~$150 of balloons? Honestly, go for it! We'll cheer for you. And if you change your mind, we're one call away.

❓ Questions people actually ask

Is this calculator rigged to make me hire you?

Nope — and that would be bad business. When your event is small enough that DIY genuinely wins, the tool says so and even suggests what to buy. We'd rather earn your trust (and your referral) than your money once.

Why is a pro garland more than the balloons cost?

Because you're not paying for balloons — you're paying for someone who has 500+ setups of experience: the right balloon mix for fullness, proper anchoring so it doesn't sag at hour three, delivery, installation, and cleanup. You're buying your weekend back.

Can you match a DIY budget?

Sometimes! For smaller events ask about our mini packages. But we won't cut corners on quality — a garland that looks thin at your wedding photos isn't a memory either of us wants.

Do you use real helium?

Yes — real helium for bouquets and floating pieces (the kind that makes your voice squeaky). Garlands and backdrops are air-filled, which is why they last for weeks, not hours.

How far in advance should I book?

Peak Saturdays (Sept–Dec, May–June) book 4–8 weeks out. Check the live availability calendar to see what's still open for your date.
