The Photo Album

Fifty-four tools on this site produced something you use before the party or during it — a plan, a budget, a timeline, a seating chart, a card for your pocket. Not one of them has ever dealt with the thing you are left holding the morning after: nine hundred photos, spread across four phones, two group chats and a photographer’s download link. The Photo Album is the site’s first post-party tool. It sorts the pile into the twelve moments that actually matter, tells you which six to print big, and lays out a real twelve-page album you can order tonight instead of “someday.”

Step 1

The pile

How many photos came back, and in what kind of condition? This sets how hard the album has to work.

900photos
Step 2

Sort the pile into twelve moments

Isabella’s rule: an album is not a photo dump, it is twelve moments you would tell someone about. Set how many shots survive from each. The engine shows you the keeps and warns you when a moment is stealing the album.

Step 3

How the album lives

Print size drives the spread count. Six big prints and the rest small is how a real album breathes — every photo the same size is a slideshow, not an album.
