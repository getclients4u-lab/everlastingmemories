      The Big Yes — FAQ

      Does Isabella really do proposals?

      Yes — secretly. The van arrives, decor goes up, the van leaves, and no one's the wiser. Her record: 14 styled proposals, 14 yeses, and 11 of those couples came back for their wedding decor. The proposal is how a lot of her weddings start.

      How does the decor stay a secret?

      Isabella coordinates with you (and one trusted friend, max). She needs 90 minutes on site and a text when you're 10 minutes out. The photographer hides in the pre-arranged spot — usually the same corner as the dessert table, the second-most-photographed spot at any event.

      What if it rains?

      That's what the Rain Plan is for. Isabella checks the 10-day forecast before she loads the truck, and she'll suggest the indoor swap days ahead. She's moved three proposals inside and all three still ended in yes.

      When should we book?

      Golden hour is the magic twenty minutes before sunset — and it books fast. Lock your date at least 3–4 weeks out; peak season (spring and fall) needs 6–8 weeks. Reserve your date to hold it.

      What if I can't find the words?

      The moment card above writes them for you — three tones, your names, your memory. Practice the first line out loud five times. Sixty seconds is all you need. She's not counting sentences; she's counting on you.

      And after she says yes…?

      You call Isabella — same design family, one less vendor to hunt. She'll walk you straight into the Wedding Workshop with your proposal scene as the starting palette. One team, from the question to the aisle.
