Every guest with a "Yes" RSVP counts as 1 seat, plus their plus-ones. So a "Yes" with 1 plus-one = 2 seats. Maybes count as half a seat each in the attendance estimate — the classic party math — and "No"s free up their seats. The capacity meter and the "Expect ~N guests" line do all the arithmetic for you.
