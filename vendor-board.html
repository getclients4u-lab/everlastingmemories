The Vendor Board

Every other tool on this site planned a thing — the balloons, the food, the paper, the drinks, the dessert. Not one of them managed the people who deliver it. A real party is six to twelve vendors, each with a contact, a quote, a deposit, a balance and an arrival window. The Vendor Board puts the whole cast on one board — then tells you the truth: who’s confirmed, who still needs paying, who arrives when, and whether your load-in is about to become a traffic jam.

Wait — a vendor board? The balloon company?

The balloons are the part you see. The vendors are the part that decides whether you see them on time. Isabella has stood in enough parking lots at 2pm waiting on a tent crew to know the decor is only as good as the load-in. This page is the board she keeps on her clipboard: the whole cast, one time, one contact, one balance.

Which vendors do I actually need?

It depends on the occasion and the tool loads the must-haves for you. A wedding runs on ten: venue, caterer, DJ, photographer, videographer, florist, cake, balloons, officiant, hair & makeup. A birthday needs six. A corporate night leans on the caterer and the bar. Tap any extra vendor and it prices itself onto the board.

Why does it care about arrival times?

Because a party is a logistics problem wearing a pretty dress. A caterer needs three hours before doors, a band ninety minutes, the cake forty-five. If three of them land in the same fifteen-minute window you get a jam at the loading dock — so the board counts every arrival back from your doors and flags the collisions before they happen.

What’s a “deposit” versus a “balance”?

Almost every vendor takes a deposit to hold your date — often 30–50% — and the balance is due somewhere between two weeks before and the day of. The board tracks both, so you always know what you have floated and what is still coming out of your account. “The date you book is a deposit; the day you remember it is a surprise.”

Can Isabella just be one of the vendors?

She usually is — balloons & decor. Drop your other vendors on the board, keep her as one line, and send it over. She will coordinate her install around your load-in window so the decor goes up after the tent and before the catering, which is the only order that works.

Is this a real quote?

No — the prices are realistic ranges so the board starts somewhere honest, not a bid. Type in what each vendor actually quoted (or charged) you and the ledger becomes real. Then take it to the Cost Estimator and the Budget Balloonist to square it against the rest of the party.
