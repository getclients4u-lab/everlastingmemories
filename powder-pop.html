      The Powder Pop — FAQ

      Does Isabella really do gender reveals?

      Yes — 23 of them, and counting. Box pops, confetti salvos, powder smoke, letter reveals: it's balloon decor with a secret inside. Every color stayed secret until the moment it flew. The reveal is how a lot of her baby showers start.

      How does the color stay a secret?

      Only the envelope knows — and Isabella never asks. She builds every scene in neutral white, gold and greenery, then loads the pink or the blue balloons into the locked truck the morning of the reveal. Even her crew doesn't see the color until it's in the box.

      What if it rains?

      Box pops and letter reveals live happily indoors — that's the Rain Plan special. Powder smoke needs calm air, so Isabella checks the wind the morning of and swaps smoke for confetti cannons if the sky argues. The reveal happens on your day, either way.

      When should we book?

      Golden hour is the magic twenty minutes before sunset — and it books fast. Lock your date at least 2–3 weeks out; peak season (spring and fall) needs 4–6 weeks. Reserve your date to hold it — and if the baby's coming sooner, call anyway: her record is a 48-hour turnaround.

      What if the big sibling blurts it out?

      That's the best-case scenario — most parents want the sibling to find out first, on camera, 10 minutes before the guests arrive. If you'd rather keep the secret from the tiny spy too, Isabella's crew runs the 'decoy box' trick: a practice pop in the wrong color the night before.

      And after the color flies…?

      The shower! Same truck, same team. Isabella will roll the reveal palette straight into your baby shower estimate — and if it's a girl, the pink is already paid for. One team, from the pop to the party.
