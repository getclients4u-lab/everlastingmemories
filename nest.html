      The Nest — FAQ

      Does Isabella really do baby showers?

      Yes — 40+ of them, and counting. Mom-to-be backdrops, nest arches, dessert-table clouds, sprinkle sets: it's balloon decor with the guest of honor in the middle. The shower is how a lot of her gender reveals end — same truck, same team, the Powder Pop included.

      How does the surprise stay a surprise?

      Only the host knows — and Isabella never asks the mom a thing. She builds every scene in sage, butter and cream, and the theme stays in the group chat until the walk-in. Even her crew doesn't see the full nest until it's up.

      What if it rains?

      Backdrops and dessert-table nests live happily indoors — that's the Rain Plan special. If the sky argues, Isabella swaps the garden arch for a doorway nest and the party moves inside without missing a beat.

      When should we book?

      Shower season books fast — lock your date 2–3 weeks out, and 4–6 weeks in peak season. Reserve your date to hold it. And if the baby's coming sooner than planned, call anyway: her record is a 48-hour turnaround.

      Shower or sprinkle — what's the difference?

      A sprinkle is baby #2 or #3: smaller, sweeter, and the guest of honor already has the gear. Isabella's Sprinkle Set is the mini nest — the backdrop, the cloud and the letters, half the balloons, all the love. Tell her which one you're planning and she'll size it to match.

      And after the shower…?

      The first birthday! Same truck, same team. Isabella can roll the nest palette straight into your birthday estimate — and the thank-you notes practically write themselves with the After-Glow. One team, from the nest to the nursery.
