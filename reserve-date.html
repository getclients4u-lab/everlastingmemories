💡 Planning ahead?

Peak season (September–December and May–June) books fastest. Popular Saturdays are usually limited 4–6 weeks out.

💍 Weddings — book 4–8 months ahead. Peak Saturdays go first, so the earlier the better.
🎂 Birthdays — 2–4 weeks is usually fine, but weekends in peak season fill up fast.
💼 Corporate — 4–8 weeks ahead. Weekdays are often wide open and easier to lock in.
🐾 Baby showers — 3–6 weeks ahead. Popular with our organic blush & boho looks!

❓ Questions?

Is the calendar really live?

Yes — this page reflects real-time availability. Isabella updates it as bookings are confirmed, so the status you see is the status we're working with.

Does reserving cost anything?

No. Requesting a date is completely free and non-binding. Once you confirm the design and quote, a small deposit holds your date firmly.

What if my date is already reserved?

Tap a nearby open date, or send us a message anyway — cancellations happen, and we keep a waitlist for popular dates. You'll be the first to know if it opens.

Can I book for next year?

Absolutely. The calendar extends 18 months out, and planning early is the best way to get exactly the Saturday you want.
