      The Launch Pad — FAQ

      Does Isabella really do corporate events?

      Yes — structured garlands are literally built for "corporate events, product launches, and formal celebrations" (her words on the services page). The Launch Pad is the first tool dedicated to that side of her notebook: same truck as the Smash Hit, but the dress code changes and the balloons match your brand guidelines instead of a nursery theme.

      Why is everything priced per guest?

      Because that's the number the person signing the invoice understands fastest. A room that totals $3,300 for 120 people reads as "about $28 a head" — cheaper than the catering, and the photo wall outlives the canapés. Isabella itemizes everything in writing within 24 hours anyway; the per-guest figure is just the translation layer between decor and the budget review.

      Can you really use our logo?

      Yes — logo walls and logo accents are standard, with one rule: send the vector file (AI/SVG/PDF) with a transparent background at least 48 hours ahead. A stretched, pixelated PNG logo is the single thing Isabella won't defend in the group photo. Brand colors get matched in latex within the limits of physics and helium.

      When should a company book for the holidays?

      Office holiday parties book fastest: December dates are mostly gone by mid-November, and Isabella's rule of thumb is to book by Halloween for December and by early September for November. Product launches and grand openings need 3–4 weeks for the logo approval + design pass. If your date is closer than that, call anyway — her record turnaround is 48 hours.

      What if the office has rules about decor?

      She's heard them all. Fire marshal clearances, no open flames, HR-approved wording, glitter bans — the office-proof rules above are the real checklist from her corporate installs. She installs after hours, keeps every exit and aisle clear, and leaves the espresso machine exactly where the espresso machine lives.

      What goes with a corporate build?

      The Party Playlist Studio sets the room's soundtrack, the Golden Hour Booth gives the photo wall a job, the Party Day Timeline schedules the agenda, and the After-Glow writes the client thank-yous. Companies rebook — and when they do, the Refer & Earn credit applies to them too.
