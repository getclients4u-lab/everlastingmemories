❓ Questions people actually ask

Will the balloons really match my photo?

Within the limits of balloon manufacturing, yes — impressively close. Premium latex comes in a wide shade range, and for exact matches we blend tones in organic garlands. We'll also confirm the palette with you before any balloon is inflated.

Does my photo get uploaded anywhere?

No. Palette Snap reads the image entirely in your browser using canvas. Nothing is sent to a server — the only thing that travels is the lead form you choose to submit (and even then, just the hex codes, not the photo).

What if my photo has too many colors?

We extract the 5 most dominant colors and drop the noise. If a color is missing, hit "Surprise Me" for alternate takes, or just tell Isabella in the notes — she'll tune the palette with you for free.

Can you match a specific paint swatch or brand color?

Yes — if you have a hex code (from a brand guide, paint chip, or website), paste it in the notes and we'll build around it. Palette Snap is just the fastest way to get there from a photo.

Is this really free?

100% free, no account, no watermark, no email required to use the tool. It exists because a client once said "I wish someone could just match my invitation" — so we built it.
