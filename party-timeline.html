    Run of show, decoded

    It's the entertainment industry's name for the hour-by-hour schedule of an event — who does what, and when. Wedding planners live by it, TV producers live by it, and Isabella lives by it: after 200+ decorated events, she's watched enough parties drift 45 minutes late to know the schedule is the difference between a good party and a great one.
    Every template is built from how she actually runs events: cake 45 minutes after dinner (not before), golden hour photos in the last hour before sunset, the balloon drop as the finale — never the middle. The tips on each moment are real notes from her notebook. That's the whole point: 200+ events of timing wisdom, free.
    Tap Edit: shift any moment ±15 minutes, hide the ones you don't need, or add your own custom moments (that surprise dance-off? it deserves a slot). Your changes are saved in your browser, so the timeline you build today is the one that opens on party day.
    Yes — that's the secret weapon. Set the party date, then open this page on the day: a LIVE bar highlights the moment happening now, tells you what's up next, and chimes (WebAudio, mute-able) when it's time to move. No phone alarms, no "was that 8 or 9?".
    One tap on Share sends the full timeline to WhatsApp — drop it in the family group, the vendor chat, anywhere. Prefer paper? Print renders a clean one-page run-of-show card: clipboard, pocket, done.
    The lead form below pre-fills with your event name, start time and headline moments — so when Isabella replies, she already knows your flow and can quote decor that fits it (and the Reserve Your Date calendar locks your day).
